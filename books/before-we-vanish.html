HEISTSIXIN

Before We Vanish

Twenty Poems

Written by Rawhaan

DEDICATION

For the people who quietly became memories before they ever became goodbyes.

— HEISTSIXIN

I

Last Light

The evening folded itself into our hands, quietly.

We never noticed how endings arrive long before the goodbye.

And somewhere between silence and memory, you stayed.

II

Winter Letters

The snow remembered every footprint except ours.

Your handwriting still survives inside unopened envelopes.

Some winters never melt.

III

Somewhere Between

There exists a place between leaving and arriving.

We lived there for years.

Neither strangers nor home.

04

IV

The Quiet Room

Silence sat beside me.

It spoke without words.

For once, I listened.

V

Before We Vanish

If tomorrow forgets our names, let today remember our voices.

For every story left unfinished, there remains a page still waiting.

AUTHOR'S NOTE

Some poems begin as whispers. Others begin as wounds. This collection was never meant to answer questions. Only to leave behind something honest. If even one page finds a home inside someone, then these words have already lived longer than I ever could.

— Rawhaan

The End

Every ending is simply another memory waiting to be remembered.
